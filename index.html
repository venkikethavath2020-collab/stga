Our Focus

Core Pillars

Four pillars that guide STGA's mission to uplift and empower Scheduled Tribe graduates across India.

Education

Scholarships, mentoring, and academic support for ST students and graduates.

Employment

Job opportunities, career guidance, and placement assistance across sectors.

Rights & Legal Help

Awareness of constitutional rights and access to legal support services.

Unity

Building a strong national network of ST graduates for collective progress.

Leadership

Message from the President

"STGA was born from a simple belief — that every Scheduled Tribe graduate deserves equal opportunity, dignity, and a platform to grow. Together, we will educate our communities, organize our strength, and empower the next generation. This is not just an association; it is a movement for our collective future."

Dr. Ramesh Naik
Founder & President, STGA
